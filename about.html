About Snaplet

Snaplet is an online photobooth website that lets users capture their moments in a fun and easy way. With Snaplet, users can take four photos in sequence to create a photobooth-style photo strip, ready to download instantly.

Snaplet is designed to offer a simple, interactive, and enjoyable experience for anyone who wants to capture memories online.
